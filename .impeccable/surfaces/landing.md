# Surface brief — Landing (/)

**Mode:** Persuade — the visitor decides and acts; the action is opening the studio.

**The one job:** earn the click through to `/app`. Adoption on the live demo is the
product's success metric; this surface exists to convert arrivals who need one
sentence of context before playing.

**Binding commitments (from PRODUCT.md / DESIGN.md):**
- Honest audio-tool voice: no medical or efficacy claims; frequency names are
  cultural shorthand; the honesty strip states this in plain words.
- The Midnight Instrument world: black chassis, the single green lamp reserved
  for the CTA (One Lamp Rule — one lamp, one action), system type stack, the
  wordmark's gradient as the sole expressive flourish.
- Fast to first tone: the CTA sits above the fold, one click from the studio;
  the page carries no interstitial (no modal, no cookie wall, no newsletter).

**Built code-first** per `.impeccable/config.json` `buildPath: "code"`.
